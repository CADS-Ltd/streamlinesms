Our Values In Practice

The Standards We Share. The Support You Can Expect.

Our values guide how we prepare, communicate and deliver. They shape every course, assessment and event we support.

  • 01

    Professionalism

    Careful preparation, clear communication and attention to detail throughout our work.

  • 02

    Integrity

    Honest advice, realistic expectations and dependable support, so you can make informed decisions.

  • 03

    Practicality

    Clear guidance shaped by frontline experience, ready to apply in real situations.

  • 04

    Safety

    Protecting people through thoughtful planning, suitable arrangements and an understanding of risk.

  • 05

    Confidence

    Helping people feel prepared through knowledge, practice and supportive learning.

  • 06

    Proactive Support

    Identifying gaps early and helping you prepare before an incident happens.

Our Leadership Team

Meet The Directors.

At Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions, our leadership team brings together frontline ambulance experience, clinical knowledge, training expertise, and practical health and safety insight.

  • Director

    Mathew Stones

    Frontline Ambulance Experience · Mentoring · Training

    Mathew is a Director of Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions and continues to work on the frontline within the ambulance service. His ongoing clinical experience allows him to bring current, real-world knowledge into the training, medical cover and consultancy services delivered by Streamline.

    With experience supporting, mentoring and developing people in frontline ambulance environments, Mathew understands the importance of confidence, clear communication and practical decision-making when responding to real emergencies. His approach focuses on making training realistic, relevant and easy to apply in high-pressure situations.

  • Director

    Thomas Wilkes

    Industrial Rescue · Workplace Safety · Training

    Thomas, known as Tom, is one of the Directors of Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions. Having previously worked on the frontline within the ambulance service, he now works as an Industrial Rescue Operative, bringing experience in workplace safety, rescue environments and risk assessment.

    Tom brings strong experience in both practical safety operations and classroom-based training. His background includes mentoring and developing others, with a particular focus on delivering clear, structured and engaging training that helps learners build confidence and competence.

Stronger Together

A Shared Frontline Approach

Together, Mathew and Tom combine clinical experience, ambulance service knowledge, industrial safety expertise, and hands-on training experience.

Having both worked in frontline ambulance environments, they understand the realities of emergency care, risk management, and supporting people under pressure. Their combined experience allows Streamline to deliver training and safety solutions that are practical, realistic and built around real-world challenges.

This shared approach helps ensure that every course, assessment and service delivered by Streamline is grounded in experience, focused on safety, and designed to give people the confidence to act when it matters most.

Mathew and Tom bring over 15 years of ambulance experience between them, with even more expertise across our wider team.
