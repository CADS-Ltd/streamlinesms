Pre-hospital career support

Your Next Step. Our Practical Support.

From preparing for an interview to understanding your next career move, we offer tailored guidance, opportunities to practise and honest feedback to help you move forward with confidence.

  • 01

    Interview preparation

    Prepare to explain your experience, strengths and motivation clearly in a pre-hospital or ambulance service interview.

    What we can help with
    • Understanding the role you are applying for.
    • Structuring answers around relevant examples.
    • Practising interview questions and discussing your responses.
    • Presenting your skills and experience with confidence.
  • 02

    Mock assessments

    Get familiar with the assessment process and practise your approach in a supportive environment.

    What we can help with
    • Discussing the assessment information provided by your prospective employer.
    • Mock assessment practice tailored to your preparation needs.
    • Developing clear communication and a structured approach.
    • Reflecting on your performance before the real assessment.
  • 03

    Feedback & development

    Understand what you do well and where focused preparation could make a difference.

    What we can help with
    • Honest, constructive feedback on your practice.
    • Identifying strengths and areas to improve.
    • Reflecting on previous interviews or assessments.
    • Agreeing practical priorities for your next steps.
  • 04

    Career direction

    Explore pre-hospital roles and opportunities that fit your experience, interests and ambitions.

    What we can help with
    • Understanding different ambulance and pre-hospital roles.
    • Discussing entry routes and career progression.
    • Considering relevant opportunities and their requirements.
    • Working out where to focus your preparation.
Step by step

How your support works

  1. Tell us your goal

    Complete the enquiry form and tell us about your experience, the role you have in mind and any upcoming interview or assessment.

  2. Agree your focus

    We discuss where you need support and agree the most useful preparation for your next step.

  3. Prepare & practise

    Work through the agreed support, which may include interview practice, mock assessments or career guidance.

  4. Take your next step

    Use constructive feedback and clear priorities to focus your preparation and approach your next opportunity with confidence.

What is pre-hospital career advice?

It supports people considering, starting or progressing within ambulance and pre-hospital care. Advice is informed by practical frontline experience and the realities of emergency-care work.

Who is the service for?
  • People considering an ambulance career
  • First aiders and FREC-qualified medics
  • Ambulance Care Assistants and Emergency Care Assistants
  • Ambulance Technicians and Associate Ambulance Practitioners
  • Student and newly qualified paramedics
  • People returning to pre-hospital care
  • Applicants struggling to secure their first role
Which careers can you advise on?

We can discuss routes into event medic, Ambulance Care Assistant, Emergency Care Assistant, Ambulance Technician, Associate Ambulance Practitioner, student paramedic, paramedic and related emergency-care roles.

Can you help me choose a qualification?

Yes. We consider your experience, objectives, time and target roles, then explain realistic routes and limitations. We will not recommend a course simply because Streamline provides it.

Can you review my CV?

Yes. We can advise on structure, clarity, relevant experience and how you present transferable skills. You remain responsible for ensuring everything is accurate and truthful.

Can you help with an application form?

Yes. We can help you interpret questions, select relevant evidence and present experience clearly. We will never create false examples, qualifications or employment history.

Can you help with ambulance service interviews?

Yes. Support may include practice questions, competency examples, values-based preparation, communication techniques, scenario discussions and constructive feedback. We cannot provide confidential interview questions.

Can you help a newly qualified paramedic find work?

We can review CVs and applications, prepare for interviews and discuss alternative routes. Recruitment decisions remain with employers, and employment cannot be guaranteed.

Can you help if I keep being rejected?

Yes. We can review the roles you target, evidence in applications, interview technique, qualifications, experience and how clearly you explain transferable skills.

Do you provide one-to-one sessions?

Yes. Individual consultations can be tailored to the type and amount of support you need.

Is the service suitable if I have no experience?

Yes. We can explain entry routes, qualifications, role differences and realistic next steps. You do not need ambulance or healthcare experience for an initial discussion.

Can you explain the difference between ambulance roles?

Yes. We can discuss common differences between roles such as Emergency Care Assistant, Ambulance Technician, Associate Ambulance Practitioner and Paramedic. Titles and responsibilities vary by employer.

Can you guarantee me a job?

No. We cannot guarantee employment, interviews, course acceptance or progression. We provide practical guidance, preparation and honest feedback.

Are you a recruitment agency?

No. Streamline is not a recruitment agency. Career advice does not create an employment relationship or guarantee access to vacancies.

Can you arrange ambulance work experience?

We cannot guarantee placements with ambulance services or other organisations. We can advise on approaching organisations and building relevant experience appropriately.

Can you advise on university applications?

We may provide general advice for paramedic science applications and interviews. Entry requirements and admissions decisions must be confirmed with the university.

Can you help me progress from FREC 3?

Yes. We can discuss maintaining competence, gaining suitable experience, progressing to FREC 4 and considering recognised ambulance or pre-hospital routes.

Does FREC 3 make me an ambulance technician or paramedic?

No. FREC 3 is a pre-hospital qualification, but it does not make you a registered paramedic or automatically qualify you for every ambulance role. Employers may require other qualifications, experience, driving entitlements, checks and internal training.

Is the advice confidential?

Personal information is handled appropriately under applicable data-protection requirements. Limited circumstances may require escalation, such as an immediate risk of harm or a serious safeguarding concern.

How do I book career advice?

Contact us through the website and explain your current position, the role you are interested in and the support you would like.

How much does career advice cost?

Pricing depends on the type and length of support. Any fee will be explained and agreed before the session.

How do I book career advice?

Contact us through the website and explain your current position, the role you are interested in and the support you would like.
