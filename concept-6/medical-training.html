Medical Training FAQs

Answers about first aid training in Swadlincote, workplace group courses, qualifications, assessments and bookings.

Do you provide first aid training in Swadlincote?

Yes. Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions provides practical, clinician-led first aid training in Swadlincote and across the East Midlands for individuals, workplaces, schools and community organisations.

What medical training courses do you provide?
  • Emergency First Aid at Work
  • First Aid at Work and requalification
  • Paediatric and emergency paediatric first aid
  • CPR, AED and basic life support
  • FREC 3
  • FREC 4
  • Bespoke workplace medical training
  • Bespoke school training packages, including support for requirements arising from Benedict’s Law

Availability may vary, so please contact us for current dates and options.

Are your courses accredited?

Where a course is advertised as a regulated or accredited qualification, it is delivered through the relevant awarding organisation and in line with its requirements. Streamline is a Qualsafe registered centre, number 908578.

What does clinician-led training mean?

Our training is shaped by current frontline emergency-care knowledge. Learners are taught what to do and how to apply it confidently in realistic situations.

Where are courses held?

Open courses are normally held at suitable venues in or around Swadlincote. Private group courses can be delivered at a client’s premises if the location is suitable for learning and assessment.

Can you deliver training at our workplace?

Yes. We can often train at workplaces, schools, care settings or community venues. We will confirm room size, access, learner numbers and equipment requirements before booking.

Do you provide private group training?

Yes. We train employers, schools, care providers, security teams, sports clubs and community organisations. Relevant scenarios can reflect your risks while regulated course requirements remain fully covered.

Can individuals book a course?

Yes. Individuals can book advertised open courses while spaces are available. Upcoming dates are shared on our website and social channels.

Do I need previous medical experience?

Most first aid courses do not require previous medical experience. Advanced courses may have minimum entry requirements, which will be stated before booking.

Is there a minimum age?

Minimum age requirements vary by course and qualification. The applicable minimum age will be clearly stated on the course booking form and made known before booking.

How long do the courses take?
  • Emergency First Aid at Work: usually one day
  • First Aid at Work: usually three days
  • First Aid at Work requalification: usually two days
  • Paediatric First Aid: usually two days
  • FREC 3: usually five classroom-based training days with assessments
  • FREC 4: usually five classroom-based training days, alongside completion of a portfolio and workbooks
How are learners assessed?

Depending on the qualification, assessment may include practical demonstrations, scenarios, multiple-choice papers, tutor questioning, ongoing observation or an invigilated examination.

Is the training practical?

Yes. Practical skills and realistic scenarios are central to our courses. We aim to build confidence, communication and safe decision-making rather than relying only on presentations.

Will I receive a certificate?

Learners who successfully complete every required element receive the appropriate certificate. Format and delivery times depend on the qualification and awarding organisation.

How long are qualifications valid?

Many workplace and pre-hospital qualifications are valid for three years. Some practical skills should be refreshed more regularly, and annual refresher training may be recommended.

Do you provide requalification courses?

Yes, where an eligible learner’s qualification is approaching expiry. Please contact us before it expires so we can confirm the correct route.

What is FREC 3?

FREC 3 is the Level 3 Award in First Response Emergency Care. It is a practical pre-hospital qualification often used within event medical work, security, high-risk workplaces and other emergency-care environments.

What can I do after FREC 3?

FREC 3 may support applications for suitable event medical and related roles and provides a possible route towards FREC 4. It does not guarantee work, and employers may set additional requirements.

Can I work for Streamline after completing a course?

Completing a course does not guarantee work. We welcome expressions of interest from appropriately qualified people, subject to recruitment, competency, insurance, safeguarding and governance checks.

Do you train schools and nurseries?

Yes. We provide suitable paediatric and first aid training for teachers, teaching assistants, nursery staff, midday supervisors and other education staff.

Do you train care-home staff?

Yes. Training may cover first aid, basic life support, falls, head injuries, sepsis awareness and common medical emergencies. Regulated content remains aligned with the required syllabus.

Can training reflect our workplace risks?

Yes. Suitable scenarios can reflect construction, machinery, care, sporting or medical risks that are relevant to your organisation.

Can reasonable adjustments be made?

We aim to make training accessible wherever reasonably possible. Tell us about additional needs before the course so suitable arrangements can be considered within awarding-organisation rules.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable clothing suitable for kneeling, CPR and practical scenarios. Bring identification, something to write with and anything listed in your joining instructions.

Is lunch provided?

Catering varies by course and venue. The course advert or joining instructions will confirm whether lunch or refreshments are included.

How do I book?

Complete the course booking form on our website or contact us directly. A place is not confirmed until all booking requirements and any required payment have been completed.

Can advertised dates change?

We aim to deliver every course as advertised, but a date may occasionally change due to learner numbers, tutor availability, venue issues or circumstances outside our control. Affected learners will be contacted promptly.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancellation, transfer and refund arrangements are set out in our Medical Training Course Booking Terms and Conditions. Contact us as soon as possible if your circumstances change.
