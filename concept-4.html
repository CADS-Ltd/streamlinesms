About Streamline

Clinician-Led. Practical. Professional.

At Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions, we combine current frontline ambulance experience with practical safety expertise to provide professional, dependable support for organisations, workplaces and events across the East Midlands and throughout the UK.

Read moreShow less

We recognise that every organisation and event is different. That is why we do not take a one-size-fits-all approach. Our recommendations are proportionate to the people, activities and risks involved, helping you put appropriate arrangements in place without unnecessary complexity or expense.

Whether you require support for a single event, a training course for your team or an ongoing safety partnership, you can expect honest advice, clear communication and a service tailored around your organisation.

General Streamline FAQs

Answers to common questions about Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions, our team, services, bookings and how we work.

What services does Streamline provide?
  • First aid and pre-hospital medical training
  • Event medical cover
  • Risk and needs consultancy
  • Workplace first aid needs and risk assessments
  • Fire safety assessments and event medical planning
  • Pre-hospital career advice
Where is Streamline based?

We are based in Swadlincote and support clients throughout South Derbyshire, Leicestershire, Staffordshire, the wider East Midlands and other areas of the UK where appropriate.

Who runs Streamline?

Streamline is led by Directors Mathew Stones and Tom Wilkes. Mathew continues to work within the frontline ambulance service, while Tom brings experience in emergency care, industrial rescue and workplace safety.

Are your services tailored?

Yes. Recommendations are tailored to the client, environment, number of people, activities, identified risks and required outcomes. We do not use a one-size-fits-all approach.

Do you offer a free initial discussion?

Yes. We offer an initial discussion to understand your requirements. Our risk and needs consultancy service also includes a free initial consultation before any chargeable work is agreed.

Are you insured?

Appropriate insurance arrangements are maintained for the services we provide. Relevant evidence can be supplied during booking or due diligence where required.
