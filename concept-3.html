What We Provide

Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions provides professional event medical cover, accredited first aid training and practical safety consultancy for businesses, workplaces, construction sites and events across the East Midlands and throughout the UK.

Event Medical Cover

We deliver high-quality medical support across a wide range of events and environments, from community gatherings and sporting events to concerts, public events and planned workplace operations.

Our services are flexible, scalable and tailored to the specific risks, audience profile, venue layout and operational needs of your event. Whether you require a small first aid presence or a more structured medical plan, we provide professional, reliable and safe support designed around your event.

Risk & Needs Consultancy

In pre-hospital care, the first step is always to assess for danger. Before treatment begins, we look at the environment, identify risks, protect people from harm, and make sure the right support is in place.

Our Risk & Needs Consultancy applies that same practical, safety-first mindset to workplaces, events and public environments. Led by IOSH-qualified health and safety advisors with real-world medical and operational experience, we help you identify hazards, understand your responsibilities, and put proportionate plans, people and equipment in place before problems occur.
