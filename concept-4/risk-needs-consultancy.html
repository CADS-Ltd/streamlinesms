Risk & Needs Consultancy FAQs

Practical answers about workplace assessments, event planning, reports and proportionate safety support.

What is risk and needs consultancy?

It involves reviewing a workplace, organisation or event to identify hazards, assess existing arrangements and recommend proportionate improvements that protect people and support the organisation.

Do you offer a free initial consultation?

Yes. We offer a free initial consultation to discuss your concerns and identify suitable support. Any chargeable work is explained and agreed before it begins.

What consultancy services do you provide?
  • Workplace first aid needs assessments
  • Workplace risk assessments and gap analysis
  • Fire safety assessments
  • Safe systems of work advice
  • Event risk and needs assessments
  • Event medical plans
  • Relevant policy, procedure and equipment reviews
  • Combined workplace safety packages
What is a workplace first aid needs assessment?

It reviews whether first aid staff, equipment, facilities and arrangements are suitable. We may consider hazards, employee numbers, shifts, lone working, site layout, previous incidents, emergency-service distance, visitors and additional needs.

Is a first aid needs assessment the same as a risk assessment?

No. A general risk assessment considers a broad range of hazards and controls. A first aid needs assessment specifically considers the people, equipment, facilities and arrangements required for first aid.

What is a workplace gap analysis?

It compares current arrangements with relevant standards, expectations or objectives to identify strengths, missing elements and priority actions.

What is a safe system of work?

It is a structured method for completing a task while controlling known risks. We can review working methods and advise on procedures, responsibilities, equipment, training and supervision.

Do you provide fire safety assessments?

Yes. We can review workplace fire safety arrangements and recommend proportionate improvements. The exact scope, including whether a specific statutory assessment is included, is agreed beforehand.

Can you help with event planning?

Yes. We can advise on medical requirements, staffing, emergency arrangements, site access, first aid facilities, communications, escalation procedures and operational risks.

Can you create an event medical plan without providing the cover?

Yes. Event medical planning is available as a standalone service. The organiser can use the plan when arranging provision, subject to any limitations or review requirements stated in it.

Can consultancy be completed remotely?

Some advice and document reviews can be completed remotely when enough information is available. Other assessments require a site visit to understand the environment and controls properly.

What happens during an on-site visit?

We may meet responsible staff, inspect relevant areas, observe activities, review selected documents, discuss incidents and gather evidence for the agreed assessment and report.

Will we receive a written report?

Yes, where a report forms part of the agreed service. It may include findings, identified risks, recommendations, priorities and suggested timescales.

Will the report prioritise actions?

Where appropriate, recommendations are prioritised by urgency and potential risk, helping you separate immediate concerns from longer-term improvements.

Does an assessment guarantee legal compliance?

No consultant can guarantee compliance simply by completing an assessment. We advise within the agreed scope; the organisation remains responsible for implementation, maintenance and specialist advice where needed.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. Our service provides practical safety and operational guidance and is not a substitute for independent legal, regulatory or specialist advice outside our competence.

Can you review policies and procedures?

We may review relevant policies and procedures within an agreed project. The scope, purpose and limitations are confirmed before work begins.

Can you help decide how many first aiders we need?

Yes. A first aid needs assessment can consider appropriate numbers and levels, including shifts, holidays, absence cover, remote areas and foreseeable injuries or illnesses.

Can you inspect first aid equipment?

Yes, where included in the assessment. We can identify obvious gaps, unsuitable locations, expired items or concerns about available provision.

Which industries do you support?

We can support offices, retail, warehousing, construction, manufacturing, hospitality, education, care, community organisations, sports, leisure and events. We accept only work within our competence and resources.

How much does consultancy cost?

Cost depends on the service, site size, number of staff, complexity, travel, time and report requirements. Current prices or a tailored quotation are provided before work begins.

What happens after the report is issued?

You can assign and complete the actions. Follow-up support, reassessment or further advice may be available when agreed.

Can you provide training after an assessment?

Yes. If a genuine training need is identified, we can discuss a suitable regulated course or bespoke session. We only recommend training relevant to the need.
