Booking questions

When is my booking confirmed?

A booking is confirmed once Streamline has accepted it and the relevant requirements have been completed. This may include accepting the terms, supplying required information and paying a deposit or invoice.

Do you require a deposit?

Deposit requirements depend on the service. Any amount and payment deadline will be shown in the quotation, invoice or applicable booking terms.

Can a service be changed after booking?

Changes may be possible but must be discussed as soon as possible. Changes to dates, times, venue, attendance, risks or scope may affect availability and price.

What happens if important information changes or was not disclosed?

Our quotation and plan are based on the information provided. We may need to revise staffing, equipment, price or scope if key information changes. In some circumstances, we may be unable to continue safely.

Do you have terms and conditions?

Yes. Separate terms apply to relevant services, including medical training and event medical cover. Clients should read the applicable terms before confirming a booking.

How quickly will Streamline respond?

We aim to respond as quickly as possible. Detailed quotations or service plans may take longer where we need to assess event information, workplace risks or training requirements.
