## How the images are classified

The brief says to use only the site's own images and nothing AI-generated. That rules out most of this site's imagery:

- **AI (filename)**: the uploaded filename says it came from an AI tool ("ChatGPT Image ...", "Codex Image ...").
- **AI (visual)**: likely AI-generated or AI-composited. The signs are garbled lettering on uniforms, ID badges and signage, distorted logos, and generic idealised scenes. **This is my judgement from looking at the images, not a fact. Confirm with the client.**
- **Text graphic**: a designed banner or card with copy baked into the pixels. It is not usable as an image in a redesign, because that copy belongs in live HTML text.
- **Brand**: logo files.
- **Badge**: a third-party accreditation or membership mark.
- **Probably genuine photo**: looks like a real, unstaged photograph.
