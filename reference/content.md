### 3. Risk & Needs Consultancy (/risk-needs-consultancy)

- **Eyebrow:** RISK AND NEEDS CONSULTANCY
- **H1:** Expert Advice, Practical Solutions
- **Intro:** We help businesses and event organisers understand their risks, meet their legal obligations, and ensure they have the right plans, people and equipment in place.
- **Eyebrow:** OUR CONSULTANCY SERVICES
- **Heading:** Safety Starts by Recognising Danger
- **Body:** In pre-hospital care, the first step is always to assess for danger. Before treatment begins, we look at the environment, identify risks, protect people from harm, and make sure the right support is in place.

  Our Risk & Needs Consultancy applies that same practical, safety-first mindset to workplaces, events and public environments. Led by IOSH-qualified health and safety advisors with real-world medical and operational experience, we help you identify hazards, understand your responsibilities, and put proportionate plans, people and equipment in place before problems occur.

  From first aid needs assessments to event medical planning and safe systems of work, we help you move from uncertainty to clear, practical and compliant safety arrangements.

  Assess the risk. Plan the response. Protect your people.
- **Consultancy services (H3 + intro):**
  - **First Aid Needs Assessment**: We evaluate your workplace to determine the level of first aid provision required. We consider the work undertaken, likely hazards, workforce size, first aid kit levels, kit storage and monitoring, first aider numbers, absence cover and emergency service response arrangements.
  - **Risk Assessment Writing**: We identify workplace hazards, how they may cause harm, their likelihood and severity, the controls already in place and the residual risk. The result is a clear, practical assessment that helps you manage risk effectively.
  - **Gap Assessment**: We assess your current emergency response capability, identify gaps and advise how to improve training, equipment and processes. Where appropriate, we can run realistic scenarios or practical exercises to test your existing arrangements and highlight areas for improvement.
  - **Event Medical Plans**: We help event organisers decide what a suitable event medical plan should include. Using recognised guidance and practical frontline experience, we create proportionate arrangements tailored to the event, venue, audience and identified risks.
  - **Safe System of Work (SSoW) Advice**: We support industrial, workplace and event clients to build safe systems of work focused on first aid and the activities they undertake. This helps protect teams, clarify responsibilities and keep operations safe, practical and compliant.
  - **Fire Safety Assessment**: We review your premises to identify fire hazards, people who may be at risk and the adequacy of your existing precautions. Our practical assessment considers prevention, detection and warning, escape routes, emergency arrangements, fire-fighting equipment, staff information and training, helping you understand the actions needed to improve fire safety and compliance.
