Website concepts

Six directions for the new Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions website, each shown across all nine pages. The logo, colours and wording are the same in all six; the layout, mood and how strongly the brand comes through are what change.

Concepts 1 to 3 are light and friendly. Concepts 4 to 6 are dark and cinematic, built around interactive pieces you can try: move a light, flip a diagram, drag a comparison, pick a course.

Every concept includes an interactive booking form demo (Book Online). Photos are the client’s own images from the current site, used for the concepts only.
