Homepage concepts

Three homepage directions for Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions. Same logo, colours and content throughout; each one uses a different layout. Photo areas are marked placeholders.

  • 01 Concept 1: Editorial split Split hero with copy beside a portrait photo, then services as a clean numbered list, an about section and FAQs.
  • 02 Concept 2: Centred statement Full-width navy hero with the headline centred and no image, then services as four cards and a section introducing the directors.
  • 03 Concept 3: Three paths Large headline over three booking paths, a wide photo band, services in tabs and a section on how Streamline works with clients.
