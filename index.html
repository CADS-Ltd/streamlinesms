Website concepts

Twelve directions for the new Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions website, each shown across all nine pages. The logo, colours and wording are the same throughout; the layout, mood and motion are what change.

Concepts 1 to 3 are light and friendly. Concepts 4 to 9 are the newest: each is built around one signature interactive idea, from a heartbeat line to an event site plan you can explore. Concepts 10 to 12 have a calmer, more cinematic feel. All twelve use Streamline’s own colours.

Every concept includes an interactive booking form demo (Book Online). Photos are the client’s own images from the current site, used for the concepts only.
