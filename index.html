Website concepts

Nine directions for the new Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions website, each shown across all nine pages. The logo and wording are the same throughout; the layout, mood and how strongly the brand comes through are what change.

Concepts 1 to 3 are light and friendly. Concepts 4 to 6 are dark and cinematic, built around interactive pieces you can try: move a light, flip a diagram, drag a comparison, pick a course. Concepts 7 to 9 keep those layouts and interactive pieces in Streamline’s own colour scheme.

Every concept includes an interactive booking form demo (Book Online). Photos are the client’s own images from the current site, used for the concepts only.
