Our Services

What We Provide

Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions provides professional event medical cover, accredited first aid training and practical safety consultancy for businesses, workplaces, construction sites and events across the East Midlands and throughout the UK.

  1. 01 / 03

    Event Medical Cover

    From small local events to large-scale public gatherings, we provide professional, reliable and proportionate medical cover that gives you peace of mind.

    • Community events, village fetes and charity events
    • Business showcases and corporate events
    • Sports and combat-sports events
    • Small festivals and outdoor activities
    Book us for your medical cover →Find out more
  2. 02 / 03

    Medical Training

    Accredited first aid and workplace safety training delivered by real frontline professionals in Swadlincote and beyond.

    • Emergency First Aid at Work
    • First Aid at Work and requalification
    • Paediatric and emergency paediatric first aid
    • FREC 3 and FREC 4
    Book your next training session →Find out more
  3. 03 / 03

    Risk & Needs Consultancy

    We help businesses and event organisers understand their risks, meet their legal obligations, and ensure they have the right plans, people and equipment in place.

    • First Aid Needs Assessment
    • Risk Assessment Writing
    • Event Medical Plans
    • Fire Safety Assessment
    Book a free consultation for your business →Find out more

General Streamline FAQs

Answers to common questions about Streamline Safety & Medical Solutions, our team, services, bookings and how we work.

Where is Streamline based?

We are based in Swadlincote and support clients throughout South Derbyshire, Leicestershire, Staffordshire, the wider East Midlands and other areas of the UK where appropriate.

Who runs Streamline?

Streamline is led by Directors Mathew Stones and Tom Wilkes. Mathew continues to work within the frontline ambulance service, while Tom brings experience in emergency care, industrial rescue and workplace safety.

Can Streamline provide more than one service?

Yes. We can combine consultancy, training and medical cover. For example, we can assess an event, create its medical plan and then provide the proportionate medical team.

Do you offer a free initial discussion?

Yes. We offer an initial discussion to understand your requirements. Our risk and needs consultancy service also includes a free initial consultation before any chargeable work is agreed.

Are you insured?

Appropriate insurance arrangements are maintained for the services we provide. Relevant evidence can be supplied during booking or due diligence where required.
